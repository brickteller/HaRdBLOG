## Article I: Name and Purpose
The name of this organization shall be...
Hampton Roads LEGO® User Group (HARDLUG) Bylaws
Last revised – 06/08/2026

PURPOSE
HARDLUG&#39;s purpose, vision and mission shall be as follows:
a) To encourage friendship, fellowship, communication, and collaboration among those who
enjoy LEGO®.
b) To promote the hobby of LEGO® to the public, through showcasing &amp; outreach activities.
c) To share enjoyment of LEGO® among our Membership in all facets, including but not limited
to building official sets, building MOCs, collecting, artistic expression, designing, robotics,
photography, videography, organization, and anything else that would drive someone to seek
membership.
DIVERSITY, EQUITY, INCLUSION STATEMENT
HaRdLUG is welcoming and inclusive to everyone, and in no way discriminating against, denying
membership to, or excluding from participation any person for reasons of race, ethnicity, age,
physical ability, sex, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, political affiliation, familial
status, religion, or national origin.

ARTICLE 1 - GENERAL
● Section 1 - Name: The name of the club is “Hampton Roads LEGO® USERS GROUP”, or
“HARDLUG” for short. The club will be referred to as “HARDLUG” throughout this
document.
● Section 2 - Fiscal Year: The fiscal year for HARDLUG shall begin on January 1 and end on
December 31 of the same calendar year.

● Section 3 – Code Of Conduct: Members of HARDLUG are expected to treat each other
with kindness and respect at all times. At public events, members are expected to
behave as good hosts or guests as appropriate to the event.

ARTICLE 2 – MEMBERS: HARLUG is open to anyone that wants to be a member. The intent of
the sections below is to provide consistent definition for accounting and not to set thresholds
to exclude membership.
● Section 1 - Membership: Membership consists of those persons who: (1) are a member
of the online membership group (defined as being included in the official Google
Group); (2) have paid annual membership dues; (3) have at least two HARDLUG
attendances in the current or previous fiscal year that meet any of the following criteria:
a. Attendance at a Monthly Meeting (excluding December meeting)
b. Publicly representing HARDLUG while in attendance at Display Events
c. Contribution of a build or MOC, including collaborative builds, to a HARDLUG
event without being in attendance
d. Participation in our online communities (Discord, Facebook, Instagram, Flickr)
e. Participation in a Committee, as reported by Committee Chair
● Section 2 - Age of Members: All Club members must be at least eighteen (18) years old.
Children of members may participate as associate members while their parent or
guardian is present.
● Section 3 - Membership Benefits: Member benefits include, but are not limited to:
a. Participation in HARDLUG meetings and events
b. Participation in any online HARDLUG communities
c. Opportunity to serve in HARDLUG Committees or act as Committee chair
d. Self-nominate to serve as a member of the Board of Directors
e. Participation in LUGBULK and Project Support
f. Opportunities for additional benefits extended to HARDLUG by THE LEGO®
GROUP (TLG)

● Section 4 - Member Dues: The HARDLUG will collect an annual $24 non-refundable
membership dues from all members. Membership dues may be paid in January of the
current membership year but will not be considered past due until 30 days after an
individual’s second attendance. Any new member that joins after January 31 will be

charged a prorated membership cost for the remainder of the year. Failure to pay
membership dues each calendar year will remove that individual as a member. All dues
will be used only to reimburse actual expenses of the LUG such as build challenge prizes,
website maintenance, signage, annual meeting luncheon, etc.
● Section 5 - Communications and Online HARDLUG Communities: HARDLUG conducts
business and engages in social activities electronically. Each member over 18 must have
a valid email address. All HARDLUG communications and notice of upcoming meetings
and events shall be sent to members via the email address they provide. The HARDLUG
Discord server shall also contain upcoming event listings and serve as an online forum
for discussion amongst members. If members wish to participate in the member-only
channels within the HARDLUG Discord server, they must include the name that is
associated with their email address in their profile display name or nickname.

ARTICLE 3 - BOARD OF DIRECTORS: The Board is the body of volunteers that manages
HARDLUG. Each member has a role that requires time and effort to keep HARDLUG running.
The intent of this structure is to invest those volunteers who give their time to HARDLUG with
the authority to make decisions about how HARDLUG will run.
● Section 1 - Eligibility: To be eligible to serve on the Board of Directors (Board), the
individual must have been a member for 365 days prior to the service.
● Section 2 - Number of Directors: The Board shall consist of five elected Directors (listed
in section 9)
● Section 3 - Terms: Directors shall ideally serve for the calendar year upon election.

● Section 4 - Election and Election Committee: The election of Directors will be held
electronically and in person at each Annual Meeting, and all HARDLUG members shall be
given the opportunity to self-nominate and vote. The Election Committee shall manage
the collection of self-nominations of candidates, the electronic voting form, and the in-
person voting forms. The Election Committee shall tabulate results and communicate
them to HARDLUG membership.
● Section 5 - Resignation: Any Director may resign at any time by delivering written notice
of such resignation to the Board of HARDLUG. Resignation shall be effective when the
notice is delivered unless the notice specifies a later effective date.

● Section 6 - Removal: Any Director may at any time be removed, with cause, by a
majority vote of 50 percent plus one of the members.
● Section 7 - Vacancies: Any vacancy on the Board resulting from the death, resignation,
removal, or disqualification of a Director may be filled until the next Annual Meeting by
a majority vote of the Board.
● Section 8 - Quorum: The presence of a simple majority (typically three) of the elected
Directors constitutes a Quorum for the purposes of Board meetings and any actions
which require a Board vote.
● Section 9 – Board of Directors Roles
a. The HARDLUG President: The President is appointed to conduct the monthly LUG
meetings and report the minutes of each meeting via email to all members. The
President will conduct meetings of the Board of Directors as needed to resolve any
issues that arise.
b. The Lego® Ambassador: The LEGO® Ambassador is appointed by the community to
represent the community and its members actively on the LEGO® Ambassador
Network (LAN). Consistency and experience of the Member appointed to the
position are valued highly within the LAN. The role has a suggested term length of 4
years. Roles and responsibilities of the LEGO® Ambassador may be found on the LAN
website here.
c. The LUG Support Coordinator: The Support Coordinator will administer the annual
LUGBULK program as well as the Project Support LUGBULK program.
d. Treasurer: The Treasurer is responsible for the accounting of all funds received by
HARDLUG and HARDLUG Committees, and shall maintain transparency to Members
through a quarterly report of financial activities. A proposed budget for the ensuing
year shall be prepared by the Treasurer and be sent to all members 1 month prior to
the Annual Meeting where it will be submitted to the membership for adoption.
e. Communications Coordinator: The Communications coordinator will oversee the
management of HARDLUG communications volunteers, including the HARDLUG
website, social media, and Discord servers.

● Section 10 - Committees: Directors may appoint one or more Committees to manage
the activities of HARDLUG, such as public events and collaborative builds. Any
Committees may exercise such powers of the Board in the management of the
business and affairs of HARDLUG as is delegated to them. Directors may appoint one or
more Committee to manage the activities of HARDLUG. A Committee shall be given
specific tasking, schedule and duration.

ARTICLE 4 - MEETINGS
● Section 1 - Scheduling: Meetings of the Members shall be held as determined by the
Board, typically the second Sunday of each month. Date, time, and location of at least
one meeting per month will be provided to members at least two weeks prior to the
meeting. Additional meetings and events may occur throughout the year with less than
two weeks’ notice.
● Section 2 - Annual Meeting: The Annual Meeting shall be held in September of each
year, in conjunction with our regular monthly September meeting. The Annual Meeting
shall:
a. Elect the Board Directors
b. Approve the budget
c. Transact such other business that comes before it
● Section 3 – Attendance: Any meeting or display event of the members is counted
officially for attendance reporting to TLG. If a Director cannot be present to collect
attendance, the Board shall appoint a HARDLUG member.

● Section 4 - Voting: Each Director shall be entitled to cast one vote on all matters which
properly come before any meeting of the Board. Each Member shall be entitled to cast
one vote on all matters which properly come before any meeting of the Members.
Votes may be cast electronically. 25% of Members must submit a ballot to constitute a
Quorum for membership votes.
● Section 5 - Video, Telephone or Online Meetings: Members may participate in a meeting
by means of an in-person, video, telephone, or online conference by which all persons
participating in the meeting can communicate with each other at the same time.

ARTICLE 6 - MISCELLANEOUS
● Section 1 – Amendments: These bylaws may be amended by a two-thirds vote of the
Quorum of Members.
● Section 2 - Records: HARDLUG shall store attendance records, meeting minutes, and any
other materials as stipulated by TLG for reporting purposes.

The foregoing Initial Bylaws are hereby approved and adopted by the Members of HARDLUG
effective this 17 day of June, 2026.
